## How to study

**5 minutes:** Read the mental model, recall the key idea, and answer one interview question.

**15 minutes:** Trace an example, change one constraint, and answer two questions.

**30–60 minutes:** Recreate an example, test your prediction, and explain the trade-offs.

Try to recall answers before reading the answer notes. Use the sidebar or search to find a topic.
